**Title** Visual Prompt Studio Phase 1. 2 – Album Cover

**Prompt**

Create a stylized digital illustration in a painterly concept art style for an acoustic folk album cover featuring a farmer sitting quietly beside a weathered wooden cabin on a peaceful hill at sunset. Soft golden light from the setting sun illuminates the landscape while deep shadows surround the cabin and nearby trees, creating a peaceful and reflective mood. Tall grass and wildflowers fill the foreground, partially overlapping the farmer, while the cabin sits in the middle ground and distant mountains gradually become smaller and fade into the warm horizon, creating a strong illusion of space. Thin white smoke rises from the cabin's chimney, a flock of birds glides across the glowing sky, and a gentle breeze bends the grass to suggest motion and the passing of time. A harmonious palette of warm oranges, soft greens, and earthy browns creates unity throughout the composition, while a cluster of glowing fireflies introduces subtle variety and visual interest. The composition uses asymmetrical balance, with the cabin positioned on the left side and the bright sunset balancing the right side. The farmer appears as a small stylized figure rather than a realistic person, emphasizing the scale of nature and creating a calm, nostalgic atmosphere suitable for an acoustic folk album cover. The artwork should resemble a hand-painted storybook illustration with soft brush strokes, simplified shapes, illustrated textures, and a dreamlike landscape, avoiding photorealism and realistic human features.

**Vocabulary Breakdown**

Value: is how light or dark something appears in artwork. It is important because it creates mood and makes objects feel more solid and realistic. It also helps create the illusion of depth in design.

Space: is the feeling of depth in an artwork, using different sizes, overlapping objects, and changes in color or brightness makes a flat image look three-dimensional.

Unity: every part of a design works together to create one clear idea: the colors, shapes, and other elements match each other, the artwork feels complete.

Balance: how visual elements are arranged so an artwork feels even and comfortable to look at

Rhythm: is the visual flow of an artwork, created by repeating visual elements. It helps guide the viewer’s eyes from one area to another and make the design feel connected.
