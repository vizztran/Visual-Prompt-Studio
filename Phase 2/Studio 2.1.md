**Prompt A — Painting/Water color**
Create a watercolor painting emphasizing color, movement, contrast, and space of a lone explorer standing on a rocky cliff while facing a giant spirit dragon made of mist and water. Use watercolor brushes with the wet-on-wet technique and transparent layers to create soft edges, flowing textures, and a peaceful magical atmosphere.

**Prompt B — Woodblock**
Create a woodblock print emphasizing line, texture, contrast, and movement of a lone explorer standing on a rocky cliff while facing a giant spirit dragon made of mist and water. Use wood carving tools and relief printing techniques to create bold carved lines, expressive textures, and strong contrast while keeping the same composition.
